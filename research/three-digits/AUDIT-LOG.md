## New evidence gathered for this pass

**DefiLlama's NEAR Intents adapter, read at source** (`DefiLlama/dimension-adapters`, `fees/near-intents/index.ts`):
- *Revenue* is NEAR and wNEAR swept into three wallets from any outside account: `fefundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near` (front-end fund), `1csfundsadmin.sputnik-dao.near` (1Click fund) and `buybacks.multisignature.near` (buyback wallet). Hops between the three are excluded. Source: NEAR's own Dune query 6740088.
- *Holders revenue* is **all** of that revenue from 2026-02-23 (`BUYBACK_START`); before that date it is booked as protocol (treasury) revenue.
- So DefiLlama's "buybacks" are captured revenue relabelled by date, not observed purchases.

**Independent onchain reconstruction:**
- Every NEAR and wNEAR flow into and out of the three wallets was read from FastNEAR's transaction API: 6,803 transactions and 5,336 flows, in the new raw record `fastnear.revenue-wallets`.
- Each flow was valued at that day's price.
- Reproduced captured revenue: **$7.99M**, against DefiLlama's **$8.06M** over identical dates (2026-02-23 to 2026-11-11), a **99.1%** match on independent paths (FastNEAR vs Dune). The adapter measures what it says it measures.

**Buyback transaction path, audited on sample transactions:**
1. A 1Click swap (operator hub account `51e8f94d…`, about 2.27M transactions) transfers wNEAR inside NEAR Intents to a one-time account.
2. That account issues `native_withdraw` to the buyback wallet.

Delivery and amounts are verified. The swap's input asset and price are not visible onchain without the Intents Explorer API, so these are **verified deliveries of NEAR acquired through Intents**, not fully audited open-market purchases.

## Verdict after the corrections

**What moved toward NEAR:**
- The cross-chain valuation gap is now a range (7.5x to 13.5x peers), not one number.
- The peer comparison is explicitly conditional on valuing Intents alone.
- The dollar "net dilution" headline, which double-counted burns, is gone.

**What moved against NEAR:**
- On the executed basis NEAR's own multiple is 309x, not 212x.
- At cash-flow multiples every rung needs about half as much volume again.
- A third of the revenue labelled as buybacks has not reached the buyback wallet: $2.14M sits in the front-end fund, and $0.44M came from accounts other than Intents.
- Two "leads" (liquidity, BTC correlation) are no longer counted as advantages.

**Unchanged:**
- The ladder's main numbers on NEAR's own multiple.
- The economy map (Intents is 86% of fees and 93% of verified capture).
- H1 to H10 outcomes.
- The generated summary: a concentrated, high-volatility bet on one cross-chain product, needing no more growth than the median alternative for the same multiple, but more than the largest ones.

**Overall:** slightly more bearish on value capture, slightly less extreme on relative valuation. The conclusion stands.

## Final verdict

**Investment verdict.**
- NEAR presents a high-risk investment case supported by growing cross-chain activity through NEAR Intents, but its current valuation depends on assumptions that remain difficult to verify.
- Intents generates measurable revenue, while NEAR's native onchain economy remains comparatively small. Much of the token's potential value therefore depends on sustained Intents growth, effective token-value capture, and the market continuing to value NEAR beyond its currently measurable cash flows.
- Our analysis does not rule out substantial upside, including three-digit prices. However, reaching those levels would require exceptional long-term growth, stronger economic activity, or valuation conditions that today's data does not establish.
- NEAR offers an interesting but highly conditional investment thesis, not a proven path to $100.

**The rule-generated lines behind it, after this pass:**
- **Leads:** largest of the four cross-chain swap venues with public volume data; highest revenue among cross-chain protocols on DefiLlama's definitions; circulating supply reported equal to total supply.
- **Trails:** an observed valuation gap to the on-chain economy; market cap at 223x Intents revenue against 7.5x to 13.5x for small cross-chain peer tokens; falling L1 activity; the highest volatility in the comparison; net issuance above treasury absorption; 67% of designated revenue delivered to the buyback wallet; fees concentrated in one product.
